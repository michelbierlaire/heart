Empirical analysis of ramp preference in the case of accidents on the Metropolitan Expressway

H. Oneyama, T. Oguchi, S. Shikata, H. Warita

Conference
Latsis symposium 2012: 1st European Symposium on Quantitative Methods in Transportation Systems (2012)
Publication year
2012

Abstract

Electronic toll collection data, which capture the history of an individual’s use of the Metropolitan Expressway, are used to analyze the ramp preference in the case of a traffic accident. The comprehensive result reveals behavioral changes such as the change in ramp preference and the cessation of the use of the Expressway.
