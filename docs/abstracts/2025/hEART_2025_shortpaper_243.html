Sampling Weekly Activity Schedules to Analyze Shopping Behavior under the 15-Minute City Concept

Georgia Charalampidou, Gunnar Flötteröd, Yusak Susilo

Conference
hEART 2025: 13th Symposium of the European Association for Research in Transportation (2025)
Publication year
2025

Abstract

In this research we present a sketch-planning tool for examining how changes in the local environment influence individuals’ shopping activity participation and location choice. We consider four scenarios with different built environment configurations and time constraints in a Viennese region, to investigate how travel behavior and activity participation adapt to these setups.
